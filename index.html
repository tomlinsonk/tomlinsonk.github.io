I’m a Senior Researcher at Microsoft Research in the AI Interaction and Learning group, where I study AI capabilities, based on real-world usage, theoretical modeling, and experimentation. I’ve also worked more broadly on preferences and decision-making using algorithmic and machine learning methods, including in the areas of discrete choice, voting, and recommender systems.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at Cornell University in 2024, where I was fortunate to be advised by Jon Kleinberg. During grad school, I interned at Microsoft Research and at Microsoft’s Office of Applied Research. In Winter and Spring 2023, I was a visiting instructor at Carleton College teaching Data Structures and Mathematics of Computer Science.

When away from my desk, I spend my time flying, playing guitar, building 8-bit computers, playing video games, biking, listening to music, flying quadcopters, bouldering, and playing pool. I also like spaceflight, Premier League football, and Formula 1.
